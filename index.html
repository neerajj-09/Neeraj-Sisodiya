About

Curious by default, deliberate by design

I'm Neeraj — a BCA student at SAGE University and a web developer based in Indore, India. Most of my time goes into building for the web and exploring how far AI‑assisted development can go: designing prompts, directing AI coding tools, and shaping what they produce into something clean and intentional. I enjoy the whole arc of an idea — rough sketch, working prototype, polished release.

My approach is simple: understand the problem before touching the code, keep the scope honest, and iterate in the open. I treat AI as a power tool, not a shortcut — every output gets a human review pass, and everything I ship has to make sense to me first. Right now I'm focused on sharpening my fundamentals and turning personal projects into proof of what I can do.
